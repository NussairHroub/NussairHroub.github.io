## About Me

I am an MS/PhD student in Computer Science at KAUST, where I am part of the [Vision-CAIR](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/vision-cair) group. My work sits between vision-language models and action: instruction-conditioned trajectory generation for autonomous driving, bi-manual vision-language-action policies for robotic manipulation, and egocentric video. A recurring theme is interpretability, since I would rather build a model whose decisions can be inspected than one that is only measured. Code from some of my projects is on [GitHub](https://github.com/NussairHroub).

Before KAUST I earned a B.S. in Computer Engineering with a concentration in Artificial Intelligence at KFUPM, graduating with First Honors. I started research as a deep learning intern at the KFUPM-SDAIA Joint Research Center for Artificial Intelligence, comparing convolutional networks and vision transformers for chest X-ray diagnosis and studying the class activation maps behind their predictions; that work became my first journal paper. I then spent February to August 2025 as a research assistant at KAUST, contributing to an autonomous driving system published at WACV and to bi-manual vision-language-action manipulation policies. Competitive programming shaped much of my time at KFUPM as well: I led my team to first place at the Gulf Programming Contest 2023, and later coached and trained the university's competition teams.

## Research Interests

- **Vision-Language-Action Models:** bi-manual robotic manipulation policies, grounding natural-language instructions in robot behavior
- **Autonomous Driving:** instruction-conditioned trajectory generation, safety-aligned and interpretable motion prediction
- **Explainable Deep Learning:** class activation mapping, explainability for medical imaging and diagnostic systems
- **Computer Vision:** egocentric vision, representation learning, video understanding
