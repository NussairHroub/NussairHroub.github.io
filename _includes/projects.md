## Projects

<div style="display:flex; justify-content:space-between; align-items:baseline; margin:0 0 4px;">
  <h3 class="entry" style="margin:0;">ML-Powered Predictive Maintenance System for Pump Systems</h3>
  <span style="font-size:90%;">Jan. 2024 &ndash; Jun. 2024</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:90%; margin:0 0 6px;">Senior Design Project, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals</div>

I led the development of a machine learning system that detects faults in industrial pump systems early, so that operators can act on a developing failure before it becomes a shutdown. I worked with industry experts to assess needs and translate them into concrete requirements, which meant the system had to fit existing maintenance workflows and satisfy regulatory compliance across several industries rather than only perform well offline.

<div style="display:flex; justify-content:space-between; align-items:baseline; margin:0 0 4px;">
  <h3 class="entry" style="margin:0;">Smart Fruit Grading System</h3>
  <span style="font-size:90%;">Sept. 2023 &ndash; Dec. 2023</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:90%; margin:0 0 6px;">King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals</div>

I designed a conceptual system that sorts fruit into high, medium, and low quality categories from features extracted from each item. Most of the work went into the system architecture, the choice of features, and the quality assessment methodology that had to justify each grade. The harder part was the integration: getting the hardware and software components to act as one pipeline without losing reliability at the seams.

<div style="display:flex; justify-content:space-between; align-items:baseline; margin:0 0 4px;">
  <h3 class="entry" style="margin:0;">Video-Based Sign Language Recognition Using Transformer Architecture</h3>
  <span style="font-size:90%;">Nov. 2022 &ndash; Dec. 2022</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:90%; margin:0 0 6px;">King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals</div>

I built a system that recognizes sign language from video, using VGG16 to extract per-frame features and a Transformer to model the sequence over time. I implemented the pipeline end to end, from preprocessing through feature extraction, sequence modeling, and classification. The two things that made it difficult were handling video sequences of varying length and getting the model to hold up across many classes.
